About

We craft elegant paper gift boxes and resin artworks — each piece designed with a soft lavender-inspired aesthetic. Every product is handmade with care, focusing on fine finishing, beautiful detailing, and thoughtful packaging to make your moments special.

Want something unique? We offer full customization for names, messages, colors, and styles. If you're looking for a personalized gift that feels truly yours, feel free to knock us anytime for custom orders! We love bringing your ideas to life.
